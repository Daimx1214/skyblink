Data & resources

SkyBlink stands entirely on public archives and open tools. Below are the sources we used or reviewed, each with what it was actually used for. All imagery on this site is a real archive product — nothing is simulated, and no frame is AI-generated.

The challenge's eight resources — and how each was used

ResourceWhat it isHow SkyBlink used it
IRSA SPHEREx archiveThe mission archive: Level-2 images, SIA/TAP services, release docs. Releases used: DR1 Spectral Images (doi:10.26131/IRSA678), DR1 All-Sky Spectral Cube (doi:10.26131/IRSA679), QR3 (doi:10.26131/IRSA662)The data itself — every frame, epoch date, quality flag and the 3I/ATLAS observation table
SPHEREx tutorial notebooks (IRSA)Official worked examples for SPHEREx data, linked from the IRSA SPHEREx pagesThe cutout recipe, spectral-WCS pattern and photometry approach our pipeline follows
WiseViewThe established WISE blink comparator (Caselden et al.)The interaction model we studied and adapted — blink interval, epoch stepping, difference imaging (see Methods)
Gaia at MAST / ESA Gaia archiveGaia DR3 astrometry: positions, proper motionsReference stars, per-epoch overlay, Barnard's Star centring and the Case 3 measurement baseline
CSA NEOSSatCanada's asteroid-tracking microsatelliteReviewed. Not integrated in this build. Idea for future work: a moving-object (asteroid) comparison against NEOSSat frames
CADC NEOSSat community archiveNEOSSat data access via CADCReviewed as the access route for NEOSSat data. Not used for any frame on this site

Access

Built to be usable by anyone, anywhere: full keyboard control of the explorer (Space to play, arrow keys to step, 1/2/3 for modes, G for the Gaia overlay, W for the WISE overlay), a colour-blind-safe blue–orange difference palette, dark and light themes, a layout that works on phones, and no accounts, no tracking, no cost. The guided tour on the home page walks a first-time visitor through a complete hunt in about 35 seconds.
