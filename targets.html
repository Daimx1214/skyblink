The five official test targets

NASA's “Planet X and SPHEREx” challenge names five objects to test a blink tool against: two high-proper-motion brown dwarfs, the fastest-moving star, and two variable stars. Every one is pre-loaded here with real SPHEREx epochs from Data Release 1 and Quick Release 3 — one uniformly processed set, never mixed with superseded releases. Open a dossier to blink it, chart its detector bands, and see it in the full-sky context viewer.

Why these five?

  1. Movers. Barnard's Star, WISE 0855 and Epsilon Indi B cross the sky measurably between survey visits — the blink test for anything planet-like.
  2. Changers. RW Aurigae and R Draconis stay in place but change brightness — the blink test for variable and transient events.
  3. Honesty checks. WISE 0855 is nearly invisible at short wavelengths, and Barnard's Star saturates its core — the set tests whether a tool shows limits instead of inventing signal.
